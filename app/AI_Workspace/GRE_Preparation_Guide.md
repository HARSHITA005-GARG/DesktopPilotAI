# GRE Preparation Guide

## Introduction
This document provides a structured approach to preparing for the GRE (Graduate Record Examination).

## Key Sections
1. **Study Plan**
   - Allocate 2-3 months for focused preparation
   - Dedicate 10-15 hours/week

2. **Quantitative Reasoning**
   - Practice algebra, geometry, and data analysis
   - Use official ETS practice questions

3. **Verbal Reasoning**
   - Build vocabulary with flashcards
   - Analyze reading passages systematically

4. **Analytical Writing**
   - Practice timed essay writing
   - Study sample high-scoring responses

## Resources
- Official ETS Guide
- Magoosh Practice Questions
- Khan Academy for math review

## Practice Tests
Take 3+ full-length tests under timed conditions. Analyze mistakes thoroughly.

## Final Tips
- Review error logs weekly
- Focus on weak areas first
- Get 7-8 hours of sleep before test day
